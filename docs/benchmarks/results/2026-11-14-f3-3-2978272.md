## Summary and limits

- Direct call: p50 `6 ns/call`, p95/p99/max `6 ns/call`; per-call values are
  integer-truncated from short batches and are near timer resolution.
- One-shot dispatch and return: p50 `1210 ns/call`, p95/p99/max observed
  `1890 ns/call`, minimum `1122 ns/call`, spread `768 ns/call`.
- Dividing the median by two gives an estimated `605 ns/transfer`; this is not
  an isolated switch measurement.
- `run_once` includes trampoline, `catch_unwind`, lifecycle transitions and
  first stack touch. The direct entry is not semantically equivalent, so no
  speedup ratio is inferred. This is a one-shot Linux x86_64 baseline, not a
  scheduler or yield/resume benchmark.
- The `powersave` governor and lack of CPU pinning can affect the spread. Treat
  this as an initial reference, not a performance claim.
